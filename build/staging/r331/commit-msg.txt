Round 331: the fluyt's fore and main topsail yards hang where Hollar's 1647 etching of a fluyt under sail (Navis Mercatoria Hollandica, vulgo VLIET) draws them. Until this round each was set at the class 0.88 of its topmast, which no plate set. Each yard is the head edge of the set topsail, traced and crossed with the mast's axis: the fore at 0.9115 of the height from the mast's foot to the topmast's head, the main at 0.8946. Built, the fore topsail yard is 15.59 m over the mast's foot where it was 16.15 m, and the main 18.37 m where it was 19.38 m. The plate crosses no yard on either topgallant mast, so hoistRead.topgallantMast answers unread with that reason and the yard stays at the class figure.
