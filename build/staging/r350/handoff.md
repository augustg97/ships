**THE DECISION.** The model keeps Steel's 3 1/2 inches on the mizzen topmast. Steel's rule was written for the British 74 that this hull is. *Asow* was a Russian ship of 1826, painted in 1828, and a painter may draw three heads alike. The difference is 0.54 m on a 44 m rig, and the record now says so as a field (rule 9).

**NEW AUDIT RULE: A-TOPMAST-HEAD-READ.** It applies to every mast whose record carries `topmastHeadRead`. The topmast's head record must carry the read with the same share, bound and verdict. The read's share must equal its own rows' (crosstrees − cap) / (lower cap − crosstrees), to 0.002. The drawn share is computed from the head records: the topmast head over the span from the lower cap (the lower head record's `topY` + `headM`) to the crosstrees (`crossY`). The verdict must be `agrees` when the drawn share lies within the bound of the plate's, and `contested` when it does not.
