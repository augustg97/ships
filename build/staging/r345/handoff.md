**NEW AUDIT RULES.** The bar-capstan rule (r172) now runs once per machine. Meshes are grouped by the tagged group that owns them, and each group is judged against the record's nearest station. Before this, two machines read as one with 12 whelps and 28 bars.
- **A-CAPSTAN-STATIONS**: the number of capstans built equals the record's stations, and each one's centre is within 0.3 m of (atU − 0.5)·L.
- **A-CAPSTAN-CLEAR**: no part other than rope stands in a capstan's drum (the drumhead's radius, from deck to drumhead top) or in the sweep of its bars (out to the farthest bar vertex, between the bars' lowest and highest vertex). The first version tested vertices only, and it passed the bars through the forecastle's end bulkhead, a single quad whose four corners all lie outside the sweep.
