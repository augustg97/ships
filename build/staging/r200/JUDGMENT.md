## The 2021 Mado 시굴 report (2018–2019 seasons, 233 finds): NO for the junk

pair, which would reopen the junk's scale contest on a record? **NO.** The
stone catalogue (마도18-xx / 마도19-xx, entries 176–191, sixteen stones with
dimensions) is Korean grooved lashed-form throughout — 홈 top and bottom,
내면/외면 terminology, no 결구부, no 두-닻채 insert stones, no wooden arms.
Largest: 마도18-104, tuff, 148.4 × 62.8 × 21.0 cm, **374.2 kg** (대형군).
The insert-form corpus stays exactly the 2017 report's three stones + one
articulated pair; **마도해역-212 remains the junk's record and its scale
contest stays closed as written in r199.**

One structural gain, footnote 64 (text line 11810): the report classifies its
stones by 홍광희 2012's thesis bands — 소형 5–38 kg / 38–105 cm, 중소형
44–98 kg / 84–138 cm, 중대형 100–292 kg / 97–225 cm, **대형 304–590 kg /
146–263 cm**. That is a corpus-grounded size ladder for the Korean form, one
step better sourced than the 2021 exhibition's "over 2 m, 300–700 kg" the
panokseon's provenance currently cites for its large class.

## Myeongnyang Ⅱ (3–5차), mined at the wire: the second arm is a FRAGMENT

**진도Ⅱ-328, 닻가지** (catalogue p. 336; A63, 2017-06-05): **길이 59.4 cm,
두께 9.2 cm** — the entry's own text calls it 목제닻을 구성하는 일부분, a

And Ⅲ names the insert-form witness Ⅰ already holds: **진도-621**, the one
