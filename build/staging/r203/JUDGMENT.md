## The excavation report (2025): what Songho-ri 1 is

A Goryeo coaster found in the intertidal zone at 송호해수욕장, Haenam, in May
2023 by a drone-flying private researcher; excavated 86 days, 2023-06-26 to
09-19. The 15th ancient ship excavated in Korean waters, the 11th Goryeo
ship. Remnant hull 13.4 × 4.7 m (port strakes 2, starboard 3, bottom 7
rows); flat-bottomed, 가룡목 cross-beams, treenails — 전통 한선 throughout —
but the WHOLE hull joined 쪽매방식 (butt-jointed, no clinker rabbet), a
first among excavated Korean hulls, and mast-partner holes (구레짝) cut in
the two rows FLANKING the central bottom plank at 72 cm spacing (7× the
대부도2호선's spacing — a big mast). Rudder found off the hull: 5.74 × 0.51 m.
Hull plank radiocarbon: AD 1021–1158 (Beta Analytic, 2σ; CAL reads
1040–1220), and the dendro study fells 우저판1-2 — bark still on — at
AD 1055–1085, so the hull's timber is third-quarter 11th century. 17
artefacts total. The report names two MORE hulls found
nearby awaiting work: 해남 화봉리선 and 송호리2호선.

## The anchor — the round's find

Catalogued p. 160 (read by eye off the rendered plate, anchor-page-161.png,
numbers verified word for word): **닻, 최대길이 149.5 cm, 최대 폭 91.3 cm,
최대두께 19.1 cm, 무게 28 kg** — found INSIDE the hull, port side near the
stern. One timber cut as 닻채 and 닻가지 together — a long shank with one
curved fluke horn rising off the end — made to be joined face-to-face with
an identical opposite half to form one anchor; three through-stations in
the shank; between the lower two, a 'ㄷ'-section groove the report reads as
the stone's joint (닻돌 결합부(추정), photographed). The analysis report's
species table (its sample 42, named 닻가지) reads it 상수리나무류 — oak.

**This is the two-shank stone-insert form — 마도해역-212's class — with a
PRINTED whole-timber record, recovered ABOARD a dated hull.** The three
census-bearing agreements, each verified on the plates:

1. Form: one timber = shank-half + fluke horn; identical halves joined;
   stone seated between the lower stations. 212's schematic exactly.
2. Stations: THREE, and the photographs (anchor-page-162.png) show them as
   SQUARE MORTISES through the timber — 212's own three square mortises.
3. Length: printed 149.5 cm against the 1.49 m r199 measured off 212's
   1/10 drawing — 0.3%.

And the carrier is dated AD 1021–1158 — Xu Jing's own century (1123), on
the same west/south-coast road at its Haenam turn. The r199 drawn object
(the kezhou's foredeck anchor) was measured from an anchorage find with no
hull; the form now has a dated shipboard carrier and a printed length.

CONTESTED WITHIN THE REPORT, named per rule 9: the survey chapter
(선체·유물 p. 77 text) calls the three stations 나무못 구멍 (peg holes) and
the catalogue calls them 관통 구멍 for 줄로 묶어 (through-holes, rope-bound
halves); 212's own joint is cross battens tenoned through the mortises. The
physical holes photograph as square mortises; the fastening reading varies
between the report's own chapters and is carried as stated.

AND THE CARRIER IS KOREAN: 송호리1호선 is 전통 한선 construction throughout.
The 2017 마도해역 report read the insert form as CHINESE practice (cordage,
the Guangzhou pottery-ship analogue); a Korean-built Goryeo coaster
carrying one widens that reading without unseating it — the form crossed
hull traditions on this coast. Stated in both records.
