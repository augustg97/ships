Round 365: the 74's channels are laid from Steel's table, above her upper-deck ports

Steel's Folio LIII ("Outboard", leaf 592 of the 1822 edition) gives each channel
of a 74 its length, breadth and thickness, and how far its foremost end stands
forward of its mast. It puts the upper edge level with the top-timber line.
Main 37 ft 6 in, fore 30 ft 6 in, mizzen 19 ft 6 in, each 1 ft 8 in broad and
6 in thick at the side (5 in on the mizzen). Until this round every channel was
a class box 0.075 L long (3.8 m on the 74) at 0.97 of the sheer, and on the 74
all three stood across the upper-deck ports.

Eckersberg's Asow, the plate the 74's side is painted from, draws each channel
standing on the top of the upper ochre band, which is the model's sheer. Each
recorded channel now has its underside on the sheer and its upper edge a
channel's depth above it. It follows the sheer and the side's curve in plan,
and carries its mast's deadeyes along its outer edge at an even pitch. New
record field hull.channels; recordedChannelFeet and channelGeometry in hull.js.
Every other hull keeps the class channel.

Lowering the upper deck to Steel's 6 ft 9 in waist was tried and reverted: the
audit convicted the quarter gallery, whose balcony deck is registered on Steel's
stern heights and agrees with the deck at 1.5 m.

Contested and recorded: the mizzen channel's height (blank in the 74 column);
the shrouds' pitch (the channels also took the backstays' deadeyes); chains and
T-plates not drawn; the waist's depth.

Audit: new A-CHANNEL-RECORD; A-PORT-STATIONS (e) now counts channel and deadeye
vertices over a port; D-SHROUDS reads a deadeye against its recorded channel's
local top. Proof B (HEAD's builder) and proof C (upper edge on the sheer) each
convict 7 times. Final audit 33 hulls, 0 problems. r364's 30 carried frames
scored: all inside the gate (aboard-clipper's 25.4% recaptured alone at
0.005%). Ratchet part 1: shipwright 1.307%, -astern 1.416%, -furled 3.535%,
-hounds 1.816% accepted (shrouds, ratlines, channels and deadeyes only).
