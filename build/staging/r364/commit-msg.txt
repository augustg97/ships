Round 364: the 74's upper-deck ports stand at Steel's stations, 15 a side

Steel's Folio XXXII gives a 74's upper-deck ports as 2 ft 10 in fore and aft,
with 8 ft 1 in clear between them. The foremost port's foreside is 13 ft 1 in
abaft the foremost perpendicular, and the after port's aftside is 6 ft 9 in
afore the after perpendicular. These add up to 175 ft 6 in, so 8 ft 1 in is
the clear space between ports and the centres stand 3.327 m apart. r363 had
recorded 8 ft 1 in as the pitch.

The model has no perpendiculars. They are registered on the 74's drawn masts
through Folio XX's mast centres (fore 19 ft 6 in, main 99 ft 6 in, mizzen
27 ft 3 in from the after perpendicular). The three estimates of the foremost
perpendicular differ from their mean by +0.25, -0.40 and +0.16 m.

New code: hull.perpendiculars, steelPerpendiculars and tierPortStations. In a
recorded tier, HULL_FRAG cuts ports only at the recorded stations (uRecPort);
every other tier still cuts every L/26. buildGuns puts a gun in each recorded
port.

The after port lies 1.19 m under the quarter gallery: the model's stern stands
about 1 m short of Steel's at that height. The port is cut, no gun is run out
of it (coveredAft), and the record says so. The cathead's class station
(u 0.105) put its knee across the foremost port. Where ports are recorded the
station now moves forward until the knee clears the port, 1.31 m on the 74.

New audit rule A-PORT-STATIONS checks the drawn ports' count, width and clear
space, their registration on the built masts, one gun a side in each port,
and that no gallery or cathead stands over a port. Proofs B (HEAD's builder),
C (r363's centre-to-centre reading), D (a gun in the covered port) and E (the
class cathead) are each convicted. Audit: 33 hulls, 0 problems. r363's 9
carried frames are scored, all inside the gate. The ratchet is running; the
receipt will follow.
