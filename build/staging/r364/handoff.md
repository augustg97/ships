**AUDIT.** **New rule A-PORT-STATIONS.** It applies to every tier whose record carries `foreM`, and it reads the built scene:
- (a) The ports cut by the hull material, read from `uRecPort` through the surface at the tier's port height, must number `nSide`.
- (b) Each port must be `wM` fore and aft, and each clear space `clearM`, both within 10 mm.
- (c) The perpendiculars are registered on the lower masts' feet as built: each foot is the average of its foot ring's vertices. Every mast must stand within 0.75 m of the mean, and the foremost port's foreside within 0.10 m of the mean plus `foreM`.
- (d) Every port must hold exactly one gun of its tier on each side, except the record's `coveredAft` ports, which must hold none. No gun of the tier may stand outside a port.
- (e) No quarter-gallery or cathead vertex outboard of the side may stand inside a port's fore-and-aft span, between its sill and head. A `coveredAft` port must be covered by the gallery, or it is convicted. The first version of this test also counted the cathead beam's inboard end, which is buried in the side just over the port's head, so the test now counts only vertices outboard of the side.
