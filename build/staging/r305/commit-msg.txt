Round 305: every gaff topmast is stayed, with crosstrees at the lower masthead and a topmast shroud a side to the rail.

Fifteen gaff topmasts on four hulls (Endurance 2, Wyoming 6, Great Eastern 6, the steamer 1)
stood with only the truck-to-truck spring stay on them, because buildRig stayed square
topmasts only. Each now has a pair of crosstrees athwart the lower masthead (beam x 0.085 a
side, the square rig's figure) and one shroud a side from the topmast's hounds, over the
crosstree's end, down to the rail one shroud-space abaft the after lower shroud. Class rules,
labelled on the cards. New audit rule A-GAFF-TOPMAST-STAYED; the stage-4 card names the new
crosstrees where they stand. Audit: 33 hulls, 0 problems.
