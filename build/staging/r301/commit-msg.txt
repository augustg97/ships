Round 301: the Shipwright's build-stage cards stop telling steamships about canvas.

A hull was treated as powered only when it had no masts, so Titanic, Queen Mary 2,
Dreadnought and Yamato were shown the topgallant, ratline, yard and "Canvas" cards. The five
mastless hulls got four fixed container-ship paragraphs that named parts at the wrong stages
and a diesel on the nuclear carrier and the wind-driven USV.

- shipwright.js: "powered" now means "sets no sail". One dispatch, swStageCard, builds the
  cards for such a hull from the parts each stage adds (userData.part tags), and the last
  card gives the recorded machinery. Sailing hulls' cards are unchanged.
- vessels.json: hull.machinery and machineryProvenance on the nine hulls that set no sail.
- index.html/styles.css: the ship nav rides on the stage card's top edge, so a long card no
  longer covers the arrows. Its position for a one-line card is unchanged.
- audit: A-STAGE-CARD; 33 hulls, 0 problems; five proofs convict (proofs.log).
