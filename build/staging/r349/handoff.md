**THE FAULT.** For a hull that records `mastStep`, r348 placed each lower top by Steel's masthead rule. Above the lower mast the builder still used class fractions: the topmast crosstrees and the topmast shrouds' head at 0.97 of the topmast, the topgallant's heel at 0.88, and the topgallant shrouds' head at 0.95 of the topgallant. **THE SOURCE.** Steel 1794, "Proportional Lengths of Masts" (maritime.org, `doc/steel/part1.php`, the passage r348 quoted): "The head of the mizen-mast, and main and fore topmasts, 4 inches to every yard of the length. The head of the mizen topmast, and all topgallant-mast, 3 1/2 inches." On the 74 this gives the main topmast a head of 19.68 m × 4/36 = 2.19 m, the fore 17.51 × 4/36 = 1.94 m and the mizzen 16.93 × 3.5/36 = 1.65 m. The topgallant heads are 0.96, 0.85 and 0.82 m.
