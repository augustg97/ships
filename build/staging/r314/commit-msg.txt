Round 314: in the furled build the topsail and topgallant yards come down onto the caps below them, as Eckersberg's 1828 Asow at anchor shows every topsail yard on its lower cap; 33 yards on seven hulls (the 74's main topsail 13.2 m lower). Plate-read yards (Preussen) and one-yard masts stay where they are. The furled card names the lowered yards, read off the built ship. A-YARD-LOWERED convicts a set yard lowered, a furled hoisting yard left hoisted, a lowered yard through its cap or off its rest, and a card that misstates them. r313's 8 unscored frames and ship-clipper scored inside the gate.
