**WHAT CHANGED** (`build/staging/r344/scripts/r344_edit.py <web dir>` and `r344_edit_audit.py <audit-hulls.js>` reproduce both on a clean tree; no shader and no record changed):
1. **On a timber hull, an island's wall is drawn in HULL_FRAG** (`buildIslands`). The material shares the hull material's own uniform objects: sun, camera, strake count, plank length and frame spacing. Four uniforms are overridden. `uTopside` is the recorded finish (`islands.finish`, tarred on the 74). `uChequer` is 0, because the ochre bands belong to the side under the sheer. `uHGrpN` is 0, so the record's glazing rows stay on the side. `uCopper` is 0. A hull built in steel or iron (Preussen, Titanic) keeps STEEL_FRAG, byte-identical.
2. **The wall carries the loft's uv.** u is the station. v continues the shell's freeboard scale above the sheer, v = wl + (1 − wl)·y / sheer(u). That is linear in height and equals 1 at the sheer, the same mapping `readWaleV` documents for the side. So the first barricade strake is the next strake up from the side's top strake. The end bulkheads (the forecastle's breastwork and the quarterdeck's breast) carry the same v. Their u runs across the beam, so their planks run athwartships.
