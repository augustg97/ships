Round 306: Endurance's gaffs at the beset broadside's length and height; her main sail brailed, with no boom

The main gaff was 3.1 m: the class cut it to 0.72 of a boom the funnel had cut short, though
the gaff swings far over the stack. Hurley's beset broadside (RMG P00018) draws both gaffs at
0.55 of the lower mast and both throats at 0.72-0.73 of it, read as shares so the plate's
contested scale does not enter. The record carries the read (gaffFrac, throatFrac, gaffRead).

Both plates that show the main show it brailed to the mast under a gaff left aloft, with no
boom across the clear gap to the funnel. The record says so (gaffStow: 'brailed'); the builder
draws no boom and no lift for a brailed sail, stands the gaff aloft in both states and stows
the cloth along the mast's after side.

Audit: A-GAFF-READ, A-BRAILED, and the class rule A-CLOTH-FUNNEL (no canvas or spar inside a
drawn funnel stack). 33 hulls, 0 problems.
