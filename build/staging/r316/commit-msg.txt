Round 316: on the clipper, the steamer and Endurance, the yards above the course hang from the mast's own sections. The lower topsail yard rests on the lower cap, as Howes' rig fixes it; it had hung 3.1-3.8 m under that cap. Each hoisting yard is set atrip (Falconer 1769) at 0.88 of the section it rides, the height the segment rig already uses. Before this the topgallant yards, and Endurance's topsail yard, hung inside the doublings, and the furled build could not lower them; it now lowers every one. A-YARD-SECTION convicts a yard left at the plan's fraction, a fixed yard off its cap, a hoisting yard not atrip, and on every square mast a set hoisting yard inside a doubling. Each furled roll now records its yard, which stops D-FURL-BUNT from giving a yard the roll of the yard stacked 1.1 m over it. The frames are scored in the receipt.
