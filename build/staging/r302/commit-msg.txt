Round 302: the Shipwright's sailing build-stage cards say what each stage adds to that hull.

The eight sailing cards were one fixed list shown on all 22 hulls that set sail. They told the junk
and the clipper that the deck "carries the guns", told the trireme, corbita and dhow (quarter rudders)
"Rudder hung aft", and told every lateen, junk and crab-claw pole "Lower mast, topmast, topgallant —
each fidded through the doubling". swSailCard now composes each card from the parts the stage adds
(with each mast mesh's segment index) and the record's masts. The furled card names only the rigs she
carries. Stage 0 is the keel on every hull: r301's powered composer had answered it with "Afloat".

Audit: A-STAGE-CARD-SAIL convicts any card claim the built parts contradict, and stage 0 on every
hull. 33 hulls, 0 problems. Proofs OLDFILE 233, GUNS 18, POLE 8, KEEL0 32, FURLSQUARE 10; restored 0.
