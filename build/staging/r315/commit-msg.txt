Round 315: the crossjack is a bare spar on the 74, the East Indiaman and the slave ship, as Eckersberg's 1828 Asow shows it and as Falconer 1769 says the sail was "very seldom used". The builder had bent a course to it (set) and a roll on it (furled), with a course's sheets, tacks, garnets and buntlines; the 74 carries 394.5 m² less square canvas. Each mizzen's record says why (crossjackProvenance: PLATE READ for the 74, CLASS for the other two), the furled card says the crossjack carries no sail, and A-CROSSJACK-BARE convicts silence, a sail or roll on a bare crossjack, its gear, a bare yard the record does not name, and a card that misstates it. Five predicted frames accepted; the rest are scored in the receipt.
