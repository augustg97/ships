Round 329: the fluyt's three masts stand at the heights Hollar's 1647 etching of a fluyt under sail (Navis Mercatoria Hollandica, vulgo VLIET) draws. Until this round they were Steel's 1794 proportions. Her lower masts are now short and her topmasts long: the fore top is 8.37 m over the mast's foot where it was 13.92 m, the main top 11.22 m where it was 15.65 m, and the mizzen top 7.76 m where it was 13.41 m. Each height is a share of the distance between the fore and mizzen masts' feet (777 px on the plate, 21.53 m on the built hull), and each provenance names the plate, its scale, the deck read at that mast, and the contested datum. Hollar's second plate of 1647, the fluyts at anchor, draws topgallant masts with yards crossed, so the third tier stands (0y172). The builder takes a lateen mizzen's lower pole from its recorded top, so r328's mizzen topmast came down with it. A-MAST-READ takes the span's magnitude, because Hollar's bow is at the left.
