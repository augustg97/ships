The cheeks are a calibre thick and splay from the second reinforce ring's diameter at the trunnions (0.436 m) to the base ring's at the breech (0.540 m). They are 4.5 shot high before and half that behind, stepped four times over the hind half, with a quarter round before and a hollowed underside. The carriage runs from the fore truck back to the button. The axletrees are sunk into the cheeks, and the trucks stand on their arms outside the cheeks, sized by the port's height and held to Muller's 4-shot ceiling. The quarterdeck carriages' cheeks are carried 0.21 m past the rule because the model lays the gun at its port's centre.
