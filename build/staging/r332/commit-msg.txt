Round 332: the fluyt no longer sets topgallant sails at sea. Hollar's 1647 etching of a fluyt under sail (Navis Mercatoria Hollandica, vulgo VLIET) draws her fore and main topgallant masts as bare poles with pennants, and crosses no yard on either; his plate of fluyts at anchor crosses topgallant yards with the sails furled. The record lists the section in uncrossedAtSea on the fore and the main, with a PLATE READ provenance. The set build crosses no topgallant yard and sets no topgallant sail on either mast, and the topsail's lifts lead to the topmast's cap. The furled build is unchanged. A-UNCROSSED-AT-SEA tests the record and both builds; A-HOIST-READ and the halyard-leg census count only the yards a set build crosses. Proofs A, B, C, D and E find 5, 2, 1, 3 and 4 problems. The audit finds 0 problems on 33 hulls. The frames are scored in the receipt.
