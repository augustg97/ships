**THE SOURCE.** Steel 1794, "Proportional Lengths of Masts" (maritime.org, `doc/steel/part1.php`): "The head of the main and fore masts, 5 inches to every yard of the mast's length. The head of the mizen-mast, and main and fore topmasts, 4 inches to every yard of the length. The head of the mizen topmast, and all topgallant-mast, 3 1/2 inches." The mast's length in Steel's tables is the whole mast. On the 74 this gives a main head of 32.80 m × 5/36 = 4.56 m, a fore head of 29.16 m × 5/36 = 4.05 m, and a mizzen head of 28.11 m × 4/36 = 3.12 m.
